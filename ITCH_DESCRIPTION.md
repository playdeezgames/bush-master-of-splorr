## Page text

**Latest version:** https://playdeezgames.github.io/bush-master-of-splorr/ (Pages switched on November 11, 2026; also add it as an external link on the page)

**Bush Master of SPLORR!!** is a Metaphor about berries.

Yer plot is small and fenced in. On it grows one berry bush, and in the corner sits a basket. Pick the berries, carry them over, drop them in. Every berry in the basket is a jool. The bush grows its berries back, given time.

Spend yer jools at the shop, the $ in the corner: bigger hands to carry more, more energy for longer days, stamina so standing about tires you slower, a better price for every berry, and new bushes to pick from. Each one costs more than the last.

Or let the robots do it. Buy a solar charging port, then a robot to live at it: it picks, carries and sells on its own, and goes home to charge before its battery runs out. The ports only soak up sun while it is day, and it is day for as long as you have energy left. The shop also sells a faster solar rate, robot speed, bigger robot batteries and bigger robot hands. And pay down the mortgage: a cool million jools at 10% a year, charged every night.

Picking and selling wear you out, and so does standing about: the day only lasts as long as yer energy. When yer energy runs dry, go to bed in the other corner; morning brings full energy, full bushes and a little more interest. Pay the mortgage off and the plot is yers.

### How to play

- **Keyboard:** arrow keys or WASD to walk, Space for OK, Esc to cancel (or, out on the plot, for the menu: sound on or off, and starting over). Walk into a bush to pick a berry, into the basket to drop in what yer holding, into the $ to shop, into the bed to sleep. In the shop, Up and Down choose and Space buys; on the mortgage row, Left and Right choose how much to pay. (F sends feedback.)
- **Gamepad:** d-pad or left stick to walk, A for OK, B to cancel.
- **Touch:** the pads beside the screen walk, OK and Back.

Yer plot is saved in the browser as you play. To begin a fresh one, press Esc on the plot (or B, or Back) and choose Start over: it warns you that everything is forgotten and asks you to confirm. Sound effects can be turned off in the same menu. "Start over" under the screen does the same.
