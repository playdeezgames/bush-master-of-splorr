Bush Master of SPLORR!! is now on itch.io, playable in the browser. It is a Metaphor about berries: one fenced plot, one berry bush, one basket, and a million-jool mortgage.

Pick berries, carry them to the basket, and every berry is a jool. Spend the jools at the shop on bigger hands, more energy, stamina, a better price and new bushes, or buy solar charging ports and robots to do the picking for you. The day lasts as long as yer energy; sleep in the bed, and every night the mortgage charges a little interest. Pay it off and the plot is yers.

It plays with the keyboard, a gamepad or touch, and saves in the browser. Esc opens a small menu for the sound and for starting over.

The latest build is always at https://playdeezgames.github.io/bush-master-of-splorr/
